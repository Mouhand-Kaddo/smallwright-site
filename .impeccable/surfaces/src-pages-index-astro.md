## Scope

The one landing page, `src/pages/index.astro`. Mode: Persuade.

## Visitor and action

Android tinkerers deciding whether to sideload an alpha. They need to believe that only the model runs in the cloud and that the phone does the building. Action: go to the GitHub release (primary key), or read the source. Proof: the README's facts, then real phone screenshots once captured.

## Constraints

No test counts, no test-phone name. Name and URLs come from `src/site.ts`. No fabricated screenshots; the LCD run is labelled as an example. Sources of truth for claims: the app's README and docs/STATUS.md (the user accepted "the README and STATUS are the source of truth, and every claim on the page must match them" in the planning round), so Builder capabilities listed only in STATUS are allowed.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a pocket calculator, the first computer that worked entirely in your palm with no network. It refuses the dark hero with a floating phone mockup and a row of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Matte navy-black plastic body (#1A1B26) as the whole ground; a large reflective grey-green LCD panel with dark dot-matrix ink; raised slate keycaps with printed legends; one blue key (#7AA2F7, the launcher-icon blue); outside the icon artwork, blue only ever means the release; a silver back-label sticker for specifications. Dot-matrix display face on the LCD, a width-axis grotesk for legends and body.

STORY: The LCD shows what the phone does with one request; the visitor sees that the model writes and the phone compiles, signs and installs, learns exactly what is sent to the model service, reads the requirements off the back label, and presses the blue key.

LCD across the full width holding the headline and a stepped example run, with annunciators at its foot for model in cloud, build on phone, alpha.
